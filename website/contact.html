Reporting a bug

The most useful reports tell us what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and roughly how many photos were involved. Screenshots help a great deal.

We're working closely with a small number of studios during the beta. If you'd like to be one of them, say a little about the kind of work you do and the volumes you handle.

PhotoFlow is free while it's in beta. We haven't set prices yet — we're asking photographers what's fair first. If you'd like a say in that, get in touch.
