Changelog

What's new

Every PhotoFlow release, newest first. Dates are when the build was published.

0.9.0 Current beta

4 August 2026

  • New: Collage Maker. A third mode alongside albums and passport photos. Seven layouts (mosaic, grid, feature, masonry, magazine, filmstrip and scatter), six themes, and an automatic option that picks a layout to suit your photos.
  • Shape collages. Fill a heart, circle, star, rounded rectangle, or any text or number — “25” for an anniversary, initials for a couple.
  • Collage backgrounds. Solid colour, gradient, your own image, or a blurred version of one of the photos.
  • Titles and studio branding. Add names and dates in your choice of font, size, colour and position, and overlay your studio logo with adjustable size and opacity.
  • Per-photo control in collages. Zoom, pan, rotate and filter any individual photo (black & white, sepia, warm, cool, vivid), or apply one photo's treatment to the whole set.
  • Automatic photo selection. Point the collage maker at a folder and it picks the best shots using the same quality scoring as the album tool, spread across the shoot so you don't get several frames from one burst.
  • Print safety. Warns when a photo is too low-resolution for the output size you've chosen, naming the file and its effective resolution, and can add bleed and trim marks for the lab.
  • Saved presets. Store a house style — layout, theme, size, spacing, background, title — and apply it in one click.

0.8.0

30 July 2026

  • Face enhancement for ID photos. Skin smoothing, brightness and colour correction, background cleanup and subtle teeth and eye brightening, each on its own slider.
  • Hold to compare. Press and hold to see the untouched crop, so you can judge an enhancement instead of guessing.
  • Fixed: enhancement could hang the application. Adjusting a slider triggered far more work than it needed to. Previews are now around fourteen times faster and adjustments no longer stall the window.
  • Fixed: album photos-per-spread setting had no effect. Choosing spacious, balanced or dense now genuinely changes the album.

0.7.0

28 July 2026

  • Several people on one ID sheet. Add two or three different people to a single print sheet, each with their own crop and copy count.
  • Live editing of sheet entries. Select anyone already on the sheet and adjust their crop or number of copies without starting over.
  • Fixed: loading a second photo into the passport tool could close the application unexpectedly.

0.6.0

25 July 2026

  • New: Passport & ID Photos mode. Face-aware automatic cropping to standard or custom sizes, tiled print sheets, cutting-guide borders and a full sheet preview before export.
  • Start screen. PhotoFlow now opens by asking which job you're doing, and shows only the tools for that job.
  • Refreshed interface across the whole application.

Earlier builds

2026

  • Album generation: quality scoring, duplicate and burst grouping, face detection and person clustering, template-based spreads, themes and print export.
  • Subject-aware sharpness scoring, so a sharp face against a deliberately soft background isn't wrongly rejected.
  • Automatic gentle correction of exposure, white balance and contrast.
Coming next: a code-signed installer, automatic updates, and moving the heavier batch work off the interface thread so nothing ever pauses the window. Have something you need sooner? Tell us — studio feedback decides our order.
