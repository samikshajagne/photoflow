PhotoFlow

AI-powered photo management for photographers.

Beta

A Windows desktop application for photo studios. Point it at a wedding shoot and it scores every frame for sharpness and exposure, separates duplicates, groups the photos by person, and lays out print-ready album spreads. The same application also produces passport and ID photo sheets with face-aware cropping, and builds collages in seven layouts including heart and number shapes.

It runs entirely on the studio's own computer. Client photos never get uploaded anywhere, it keeps working when the internet doesn't, and a twenty-thousand-frame wedding costs exactly the same as a small one — nothing is metered per image.

  • Album generation with quality scoring and face grouping
  • Passport & ID photo sheets with face-aware cropping
  • Collage maker with seven layouts and shaped collages
  • Windows 10 & 11, 64-bit, works fully offline
  • Free while in beta
