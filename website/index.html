PhotoFlow

AI-powered photo management for photographers.

Beta

A Windows desktop application for photo studios. PhotoFlow sorts a whole wedding shoot by quality, groups the photos by person and builds print-ready album spreads — then handles passport and ID photo sheets and collages from the same library. It runs entirely on the studio's own computer, so client photos never leave the premises, and nothing is charged per photo.

  • Album generation with quality scoring and face grouping
  • Passport & ID photo sheets with face-aware cropping
  • Collage maker with seven layouts and shaped collages
  • Windows 10 & 11, works fully offline
