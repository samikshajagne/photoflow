What's inside

Pick a job, get to work

PhotoFlow opens on a single question: what are you doing today? Each mode is a focused tool, so nothing is cluttered with controls you don't need right now.

▣

Mode one

Generate Album

Point it at a shoot. It scores every frame for sharpness and exposure, groups duplicates, finds and clusters faces, then lays out print-ready spreads.

  • Quality scoring and culling
  • Face detection and person grouping
  • Template-based spreads and themes
  • Export for your print lab
◫

Mode two

Passport & ID Photos

Crop to any official size, tile copies onto a print sheet, and put two or three different people on one sheet instead of wasting paper.

  • Face-aware automatic cropping
  • Several people on one sheet
  • Cutting-guide borders
  • Optional face enhancement
◈

Mode three

Collage Maker

Choose photos, a theme and a layout, and a finished collage appears — including heart and number shapes for anniversaries.

  • Seven layouts, six themes
  • Heart, star and text shapes
  • Titles and studio watermark
  • Print-size safety checks

Getting started

From install to first album

Install and open

One installer for Windows. PhotoFlow opens on the mode chooser.

Point it at a folder

Choose the shoot. PhotoFlow reads the photos without moving or altering your originals.

Let it do the sorting

Blurry frames, duplicates and the strongest shots are separated out, and faces are grouped by person.

Review and adjust

Change anything you disagree with — it's a starting point, not a locked result.

Export for print

Album spreads, ID photo sheets or collages, at full print resolution.
